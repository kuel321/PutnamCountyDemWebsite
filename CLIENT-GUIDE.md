# What's New on the Website

A simple guide to the new features and how to use them. If anything doesn't make sense, just reach out and we'll walk through it together.

---

## 1. Home Page News & Highlights

The home page now has a box that automatically scrolls through short news items — good for quick announcements, reminders, or updates.

**To add or change one:**
1. Log into the admin panel.
2. Click **Highlights** in the left menu.
3. Click an existing item to edit it, or **Create New** to add one.
4. Fill in a **Title** and write your message in the **Content** box.
5. The **Order** number controls what shows first (lower numbers show first).
6. Click **Save**.

It will show up on the home page automatically and rotate along with the others.

## 2. Candidate Ads on the Home Page

To get a candidate's ad showing up here, see the next section.

## 3. Candidate Profiles

**To add a candidate:**
1. In the admin panel, click **Candidates**.
2. Click **Create New**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Candidate Name**
   - **Type** — choose "Federal / State" or "Putnam Co." depending on the race
   - **District** — only appears for Putnam Co. candidates, once we have district info loaded
   - **Headshot** — a photo of the candidate
   - **Content** — their bio or write-up
   - **Links** — their website and Facebook page
   - **Gallery** — upload their campaign ad images here; these are what show up in the home page spotlight
4. Click **Save**.

Note: there isn't a dedicated "Meet the Candidates" page on the site yet showing this info to visitors — that's still on the to-do list. For now, this feeds the home page ad spotlight.

## 4. Club Members Area (password-protected)

There's now a private, password-protected area of the website just for club members, currently used for submitting meeting minutes.

### Adding a club member
1. In the admin panel, click **Club Members**.
2. Click **Create New**.
3. Enter their **name**, **email**, and set a **password** for them.
4. Make sure **Access Enabled** is checked.
5. Click **Save**, then share the login page and their email/password with them: `/members/login`

### Turning off someone's access
If a member shouldn't have access anymore (but you don't want to delete their account):
1. Open their entry under **Club Members**.
2. Uncheck **Access Enabled**.
3. Click **Save**. They will no longer be able to log in, but their account and history stay on file.

### How a club member submits meeting minutes
1. They log in at `/members/login`.
2. They click **Submit Minutes**, fill in the meeting title and date, and upload the minutes as a **PDF or Word document**.
3. It shows up on their dashboard as **Pending Review**.

### How you approve (or reject) submitted minutes
1. In the admin panel, click **Minutes Submissions**.
2. Open the one you want to review — you can view/download the file they uploaded.
3. Change **Status** to **Approved** (or **Rejected**, with an optional note explaining why in **Review Notes**).
4. Click **Save**.

When you approve one, it automatically appears on the public **Meeting Minutes** page — no extra steps needed.
